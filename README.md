# Airplane-crash-and-fatalities-in-India

✈️ Airplane Crash and Fatalities Analysis – Report
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes airplane crash and fatality data to understand why airplanes crash and what mistakes or issues cause these accidents. The goal is to identify major reasons such as pilot error, hijacking, technical failure, and weather issues, and suggest actions to reduce future crashes.

🔍 Key Findings (What Causes Airplane Crashes?)

The analysis shows that pilot error is one of the main reasons for many airplane crashes. Mistakes during take-off, landing, or navigation often lead to accidents.

Technical and mechanical failures also play a major role, especially due to poor maintenance or system malfunction.

Bad weather conditions like storms, fog, and strong winds increase the risk of crashes, mainly during landing.

Hijacking and security-related incidents are fewer compared to other causes but can lead to serious accidents and high fatalities.

Some crashes are caused by human errors on the ground, such as poor communication or wrong instructions.

🧠 Prescriptive Analysis (What Should Be Done?)

Airlines should provide better pilot training and regular skill checks to reduce human errors.

Aircraft should undergo strict and frequent maintenance checks to avoid technical failures.

Advanced weather monitoring systems should be used to warn pilots early.

Airport and airline security should be strengthened to prevent hijacking and unlawful interference.

✅ Conclusion

This analysis shows that most airplane crashes are preventable. By improving training, maintenance, weather systems, and security, airlines can significantly reduce crashes and save lives.
